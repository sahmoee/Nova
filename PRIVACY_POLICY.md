# FrameTV Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [FILL IN DATE BEFORE HOSTING]_

FrameTV is a personal media player and library manager for Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad. This policy explains what FrameTV does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

- FrameTV does not include, host, sell, index, or provide any video content.
- FrameTV has no servers of its own and collects no analytics.
- Your library, watch history, and settings are stored on your device.
- FrameTV only communicates with services **you** set up, using credentials **you** provide.

## What FrameTV stores on your device

- Your library, watch progress, favorites, and app settings are stored locally on your device.
- If you enable iCloud sync, your settings and library state are stored in your own private iCloud key-value store so they can move between your devices. This uses your Apple account; FrameTV never sees this data on any server.
- API keys, tokens, and passwords you enter are stored in the device Keychain. They are never written to logs.

## Services you can connect (all optional)

FrameTV only contacts a service if you configure it. Here is what each one is used for:

- **TMDB** — Fetches posters, backdrops, and episode details, using an API key you provide.
- **Trakt** — Syncs your watchlist, lists, and watch progress, if you connect your account.
- **OMDb** — Fetches IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Metacritic ratings, using an API key you provide.
- **Real-Debrid** — Resolves links through your own account, using a token you authorize. FrameTV never receives your Real-Debrid password.
- **SMB shares** — Reads the specific network shares you add, on your local network.
- **AI Worker** — If you deploy your own AI Worker and add its URL, the search text you type (and, for library search, your library titles) is sent to that Worker, which you control. This is off until you configure it.

## What FrameTV does not do

- It does not collect analytics or telemetry.
- It does not send your data to the developer.
- It does not bypass DRM or help locate copyrighted material.
- It does not include advertising or trackers.

## Children

FrameTV is a general-purpose media player and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
