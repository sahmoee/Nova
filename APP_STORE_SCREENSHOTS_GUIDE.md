# FrameTV — App Store Screenshots & Preview Guide

Apple requires that screenshots and previews show only what is actually in the app, and must not look like you are advertising access to specific copyrighted titles. Follow this.

## Safe screenshots to capture (recommended set)

1. First-launch Personal Media disclosure (shows the app's honest posture).
2. Library grid using YOUR OWN added items, or a clean empty state.
3. A detail screen showing metadata enrichment, ratings badges, and the playback engine choice.
4. Continue Watching.
5. The stream picker showing the Recommended group and confidence badges (blur or crop any specific pirated-looking filenames).
6. Settings, Privacy and Legal (shows privacy-first design).
7. Settings, Library Health (shows the personal-media management angle).
8. SMB / local media setup (emphasizes personal media).

## Do NOT

- Do not show artwork or titles arranged to look like a catalog of copyrighted movies you are offering.
- Do not show filenames that imply piracy (release-group tags, etc.). Crop or blur them.
- Do not imply the app provides free or unlimited content.
- Do not use posters of specific copyrighted films as the hero of a screenshot unless it is clearly the user's own library.

## Preview text / description wording

Use: personal media player, library manager, bring your own sources, play media you own or are authorized to access, metadata enrichment, choose your player engine, works with your SMB shares.

Avoid: free movies, stream anything, unlimited, watch all shows, torrent, pirate.

## Caption suggestions (safe)

- "Your library, beautifully organized."
- "Bring your own sources. FrameTV does the rest."
- "Rich metadata for the media you own."
- "Two playback engines. Pick what plays best."
- "Private by design. Your data stays yours."
