Astra Privacy Policy

Personal media for iPhone, iPad & Apple TV

Last updated: July 8, 2026

The short version. Astra is a local-first app. Your library, settings, sources, and logins live on your device and in your own private iCloud. Astra has no accounts, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data. There is no analytics or tracking built into the app.

No adsNo accountsNo trackingLocal-first

What Astra stores, and where

  • On your device: your library, playback progress, preferences, installed add-ons, saved sources (SMB shares, Live TV playlists), and the record of which stream you last used.
  • In Apple Keychain: credentials you enter — API keys (e.g. TMDB, OMDb, OpenSubtitles), Trakt and Real-Debrid tokens, and SMB / Live TV passwords. These are stored securely by iOS/tvOS.
  • In your private iCloud (key-value storage): a backup "snapshot" and settings sync, so your setup follows you across your own Apple devices. This stays within your iCloud account; the developer has no access to it.

Third-party services you choose to connect

Astra only talks to services you configure. When you connect one, your requests go directly from your device to that provider under their terms and privacy policy:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) / OMDb: to look up titles, artwork, and descriptions.
  • Trakt: to sync your watchlist and watched progress, if you sign in.
  • Real-Debrid: to resolve cached streams, if you provide a token.
  • OpenSubtitles: to fetch subtitles, if enabled.
  • Add-ons you install: catalog/stream add-ons you add by URL are contacted to find playable streams.
  • Your own Cloudflare Worker (optional): powers AI search and peer-to-peer backup share codes (see below). You deploy and control this Worker.

Backup & sharing

Astra can back up your setup to your private iCloud and let you export a snapshot file. You choose exactly what a backup or share includes — preferences, sources, add-ons, and optionally your logins and API keys.

Share codes. If you enable it, Astra can create a short code that another person enters to restore your snapshot. This works through your own Cloudflare Worker and its storage; the snapshot is held there only until the code expires (by default 7 days). A snapshot that includes logins contains sensitive secrets — the app warns you, and you should only share such a code with people you trust.

What Astra does not do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data with advertisers.
  • No user accounts to sign up for, and no cross-app tracking.
  • No collection of your viewing activity by the developer.

Children

Astra is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any service at any time in Settings; remove keys and tokens to delete them from Keychain.
  • Delete backups from iCloud, and let share codes expire (or they expire automatically).
  • Uninstalling the app removes its on-device data; iCloud key-value data can be cleared from your Apple ID settings.
