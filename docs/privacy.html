Privacy Policy

Nova is local-first, includes no advertising or cross-app tracking, and gives you control over every optional connection.

Effective and last updated: August 8, 2026

The short version: the developer does not operate a Nova account service or collect your viewing activity. Your data stays on your device or in your private iCloud unless you choose a feature that contacts an external service.

Information stored by Nova

Nova stores your library, favorites, watch history, playback progress, profiles, preferences, installed add-ons, configured sources, and cached media information on your device. Service credentials and tokens you enter are stored in Apple Keychain where supported.

You can optionally synchronize settings and a backup snapshot through Apple's private iCloud key-value storage. This data is associated with your Apple ID and governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. The Nova developer cannot access your private iCloud container.

Services you choose to connect

Depending on your configuration, Nova may send requests directly to TMDB, OMDb, Trakt, Real-Debrid, OpenSubtitles, SMB servers, Live TV providers, and add-ons you install. Requests can include search terms, title or file identifiers, account tokens, watch progress, source credentials, or media requests needed to perform the feature you selected.

Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Nova does not control them. Review a provider's policy before connecting it.

Optional AI features

AI features are off until you configure a compatible Cloudflare Worker. When used, text you submit and the limited context needed to answer it may be sent to your Worker and then to its configured AI provider, currently Anthropic. Library-assisted requests may include media titles from your library. Nova does not send passwords or account tokens to the AI provider.

The person who deploys the Worker controls its Cloudflare account, logs, retention, and AI-provider account. The included Worker does not create a prompt-history database, but Cloudflare observability or the AI provider may process request data under their policies. Remove the Worker URL and token in Settings to stop using AI features.

Backups and share codes

You decide whether a backup includes settings, sources, add-ons, or credentials. Exported backup files remain wherever you save or share them. If you create a share code, the selected snapshot is stored in the Worker you configured. It expires after the selected period—7 days by default and no more than 30 days—and is deleted when redeemed or when its expiration alarm runs.

Data the developer collects

The app has no ad or analytics SDK and does not send advertising identifiers, contacts, photos, precise location, or viewing activity to the developer. The developer may receive the email address, message, and diagnostics you voluntarily provide when requesting support. Support correspondence is used to respond, diagnose issues, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations, and is kept only as long as reasonably necessary.

Apple and the App Store may independently process purchase, diagnostic, or device information under Apple's privacy policy. Services you configure process data independently as described above.

Your choices and deletion

  • Disconnect accounts and remove credentials, add-ons, sources, or the AI Worker in Settings.
  • Delete on-device Nova data by deleting the app.
  • Manage Nova's iCloud data through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.
  • Request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to legal and security retention needs.

Nova has no central account to delete. Removing Nova does not delete information held by a service you configured; use that provider's controls.

Children

Nova is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child submitted information through support.

Security

Nova uses platform protections such as Apple Keychain and encrypted HTTPS where providers support it. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect exported backups and connect only services and add-ons you trust.

Changes

This policy may change as Nova evolves. The date above will be updated when changes are published, and material changes will be described in the app or on this site when appropriate.
