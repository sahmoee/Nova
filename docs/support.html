Nova Support

Tell us what happened and we’ll help you get back to your library.

Email support

Before you write

  1. Open Settings → Sources & Add-ons → Health to check configured services.
  2. Confirm the device is online and can reach the source directly.
  3. Reconnect an expired service under Settings → Accounts.
  4. For playback issues, try the other player or another file you are authorized to access.
  5. Export the secret-free Debug Report from Settings and attach it to your message.

What to include

Include your Nova version, device model, iOS or tvOS version, and the steps that cause the issue. Do not send passwords, API keys, access tokens, private media URLs, or backup files containing credentials.

External services

Nova does not provide media, subscriptions, or credentials. Contact the relevant provider about its availability, billing, or account access.
