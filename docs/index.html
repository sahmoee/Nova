Everything in one place

Nova is a personal media hub — it connects the sources you already use and gets out of your way.

🔎

Universal search

Search movies and shows with predictive suggestions and natural-language AI — the same powerful search on Home and Discover.

▶️

Seamless resume

Pause, browse, come back. Nova resumes the same stream at the exact spot — no re-picking, no prompts.

🎞️

Now Playing bar

A floating bar keeps your video a tap away while you navigate, with play, resume, and stop controls.

🗂️

Your own sources

SMB network shares, Live TV playlists, add-ons, and cloud debrid — organized, health-checked, and ready to play.

🧠

Smart stream picker

Streams ranked by quality, size, and speed — with your last-used stream marked "used 6 hrs ago" for one-tap continue.

☁️

Backup & sync

Your setup follows you across your own Apple devices via private iCloud — preferences, sources, and logins.

Share your setup with a code

Hand a friend a short code and they can restore your snapshot instantly — no files to send. You choose exactly what's included, and Nova warns you before anything with logins is shared. Codes run through your own Cloudflare Worker and expire automatically.
