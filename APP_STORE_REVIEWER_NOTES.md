# FrameTV — App Store Review Notes

Paste the section below into App Store Connect, under App Review Information, Notes.

---

FrameTV is a personal media player and library manager for Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad. It is a player, not a content service.

WHAT THE APP IS
- FrameTV plays and organizes media the user owns or is authorized to access.
- It does NOT include, host, sell, index, or provide any video content or catalog.
- It ships with no media, no sources, and no credentials.

FIRST LAUNCH
- On first launch the app shows a Personal Media disclosure explaining the above. Tap I understand to continue.

HOW TO REVIEW WITHOUT ACCOUNTS
- The app is fully navigable without configuring any source.
- Trending and metadata sections require the user's own TMDB key and are optional.
- Real-Debrid, SMB, magnets, and direct URLs all require the reviewer or user to supply their own accounts or links. None are needed to review the app's interface, library, settings, privacy screens, and playback UI.

PRIVACY
- No analytics, no telemetry, no developer servers.
- Credentials are stored in the Keychain. Library and settings stay on device (optionally synced via the user's own iCloud).
- The in-app Settings, Privacy and Legal screen lists exactly what each optional service can access.
